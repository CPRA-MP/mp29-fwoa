<span className="subtitle">Louisiana's Coast in a Future Without Action</span>

<div className="right">
Coastal Louisiana has always been shaped by natural processes, storms, and human decisions about how land, water, infrastructure, and flood risk are managed. These forces continue to affect the landscape and how communities plan for the future.

We can use historical maps and observations to understand how the coast has changed in the past. To explore how it may change in the future to inform development of the 2029 Coastal Master Plan (MP29), CPRA uses predictive models. These models start with current observations of the coast and then test different future scenarios, or sets of assumptions, about possible future environmental conditions and planning decisions.

One important scenario, called **Future Without Action (FWOA)**, illustrates how coastal Louisiana may change if no additional restoration or risk-reduction projects are built. This site presents MP29 FWOA results as a tool to support coastwide planning.
